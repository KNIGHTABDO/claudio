# Mood & Environment Rules
- **Rainy Weather**: Play melancholic jazz, slow piano, or soft rock.
- **Sunny Weather**: Play upbeat indie, pop, or light acoustic.
- **Cloudy/Foggy**: Focus on ambient, lo-fi, or neo-classical.
- **Late Night**: Very low volume, instrumental, or "whispering" vocals.
- **Busy Calendar**: If my calendar says "Meeting" or "Deep Work", switch to instrumental focus music immediately.
